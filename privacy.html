Privacy policy

This private assistant is only for its owner. Access to a Google account is accepted only for the owner's own account.

Google data it accesses

  • Gmail: message headers and metadata, including senders, recipients and subjects, and message bodies or attachments needed for the owner's requests.
  • Google Calendar: event details such as titles, dates, times, attendees, locations and descriptions.
  • Google Drive: file names and metadata, and the contents of files selected or needed for the owner's requests.

How content is used and shared

Google data is used only to provide the owner's personal assistant functions. Content goes only to the AI and messaging services that run the assistant, as needed to carry out those functions. It is never sold, made public, or shared with other parties or for unrelated purposes.

Information needed for ongoing requests and continuity may be retained by the assistant and the services that operate it. This public information site contains no Google account content.

How to revoke access

Open Google Account connections, select this assistant's connection, and remove its access. Revoking access stops future access through that connection; it does not automatically erase information already retained. The owner can separately request removal of retained assistant information.
